# Chatbot

Chatbot is made to be used on B.a.B.e. website https://babe.hr/.

B.a.B.e. is a Croatian non government organisation founded in 1994. Its main goal is raising awareness about gender inequality and human rights violations, and prevention of violence against women. The organization organizes projects related to the inclusion of vulnerable social groups, and also offers legal and psychological counseling services and accommodation in a safe house.
